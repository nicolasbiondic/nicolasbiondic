# Nicolás Biondi

I run [LENZ Photo Store](https://nicolasbiondi.com), a Canon and Sony distributor and ecommerce in Peru, and I build the automations that keep it running with AI coding agents. I also teach and volunteer as a firefighter. Based in Lima, Peru.

Construyo, enseño y a veces apago incendios.

## What I work on

- Building systems with AI coding agents, mostly for ecommerce and internal tooling.
- Writing about what breaks and what I learn fixing it, at [nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog](https://nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog/).
- [**uncle-carl**](https://github.com/nicolasbiondic/uncle-carl): self-hosted trading platform, momentum and mean-reversion portfolios on Alpaca and Binance Futures. MIT.

## Recent writing

- [Boss and interns](https://nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog/kitchen-brigade-ai-agents/): how I split coding work across several AI models without the bill exploding.
- [The wrong key](https://nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog/la-llave-equivocada/): a dedup key pointed at a row's position instead of the camera, and duplicates walked right past it.
- [The flag decided nothing](https://nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog/el-ensayo-facturo/): test mode rode in every request and the invoicing API ignored it.
- [Midnight doesn't count](https://nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog/medianoche-no-cuenta/): our counter allowed 96 posts in 24 rolling hours, X counts it differently.
- [The silent doorbell](https://nicolasbiondi.com/en/blog/diez-dias-sin-timbre/): rotating a credential left an alerts channel silent without raising a single error.
